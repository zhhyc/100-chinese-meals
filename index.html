How It Started

2020. COVID hit. Everyone was stuck at home.

I'd never really written down my recipes before. I just cooked — wok on high heat, a bit of this, a bit of that, done. But now I had time. So I started paying attention. Actually measuring. Writing things down.

I sent a photo of my kung pao chicken to a friend in Ohio. He'd ordered it from restaurants for years but had never made it. He asked for the recipe. I tried to explain it in English. It was... harder than I expected.

So I video-called him. Walked him through it. He made it. Texted me after: "I've been eating kung pao chicken for ten years and I had no idea it was supposed to taste like THIS."

That's when I realized — the recipes he'd been finding online weren't... great. They weren't bad exactly, but they were written by people who'd never cooked this stuff in a real Chinese kitchen. "Marinate the chicken for 30 minutes." You don't marinate chicken for kung pao. Who wrote that?

So I started writing my own recipes. Testing them on friends who'd never cooked Chinese food. Watching where they got confused. Fixing the instructions.

I kept going. 20 recipes. Then 50. Then 100.

The first 5 are free. The rest... well, they're written, but I'm still tweaking them. When they're ready, they'll go up.

— Zhang Ren
